Event Command Center

Privacy policy · Kunte Studio · Updated 2 October 2026

What the app does

Event Command Center helps you plan multiple events, programs, tasks, budgets, vendors, contract references, payment records, households, guests, RSVPs, accommodation, transport and seating. It is a planner, not a booking service, payment processor or messaging service.

Information on your device

Information you enter is stored in a private local database on your Android device. It can include event dates, guest names and contact details, dietary or logistics notes, vendor information, and financial planning records, including manual income/sponsorship entries and refunds. Kunte Studio does not receive automatic uploads of this information. The app has no developer-operated server, accounts, Google or Facebook sign-in, advertising, analytics or added crash-reporting service. It requests no network permission. Device system services may maintain diagnostics independently of the app.

Security

The app relies on Android device protection. Its database is not separately encrypted by this app. A person who can use your unlocked device may be able to open the app. Use a device lock. Avoid storing bank credentials, card numbers, identity documents or other details unnecessary for event planning. Contract references are text or filenames; documents are not attached or uploaded.

Backups and external providers

Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. If you choose Export, the app creates an unencrypted JSON file containing all event projects, including archived projects and guest and financial planning information. You choose the location through the Android document picker. A provider you select, including a cloud account connected on your phone, handles the file under its own terms. Kunte Studio does not receive a copy automatically. Keep exported files private. The app cannot remove copies from external destinations.

Calendar exports and reusable structures

If you choose Export calendar file, the app creates an iCalendar file with the selected programs and agenda. It can include titles, locations and owner labels, but not the attendee register. Calendar export uses the event timezone you set. You choose the destination and whether to import it into a calendar service. The app does not sign in to a calendar account or transmit the file automatically. Event reuse copies planning structure with new IDs and shifted dates, excluding attendee records, vendors and transactions; retained titles should be reviewed for private information.

Restore and recovery

Restoring a collection backup replaces all events after confirmation. An older supported native single-event backup replaces the selected event. The app keeps one applicable pre-restore snapshot locally for undo. Later restores replace recovery; deleting a event clears recovery; deleting all data removes local events and recovery.

Deletion and retention

Local plans remain on your device until you delete them or remove app storage. You can delete eligible individual records or use More > Help, privacy & backups > Delete all data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes local app data. Delete exported files separately from their chosen locations. There is no app account or developer event database to delete.

Support

If you email supreet.kunte@gmail.com, Kunte Studio receives your email address and any information you choose to include. This voluntary communication is separate from automatic app collection and is used to respond to your request. Do not send event backups or personal guest information for routine support. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to ongoing support needs and applicable retention obligations. Your email provider processes email under its own terms.

Children

The app is intended for adult event planning and does not ask children to register or submit information to Kunte Studio. Guest records may include children when entered locally by a planner. Keep these records limited to the information needed for the event.

Changes and questions

If connected services or data practices change, this policy will be updated to reflect the actual app. Contact Kunte Studio at supreet.kunte@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.
